# BLACKTRACK — Story & Maya

---

## The Story

### What This Game Is

BLACKTRACK is a stealth narrative game set across three chapters of the
transatlantic slave trade and its aftermath. The player navigates history
not as a spectator but as a participant — through the eyes of Maya,
a being of uncertain origin who moves through time, space, and perception
to bear witness to what actually happened.

This is not a revenge fantasy. It is not a comfort narrative.
It is an attempt to tell the truth of one of history's most documented
and most deliberately forgotten crimes — through the lives of the people
who lived it.

The individual stories are rooted in documented history.
Some names, places, and circumstances have been shaped for narrative.
The human truth beneath them has not.

---

### The Arc

**Chapter 1 — The Gold Coast: Barracoons** *(Free Demo)*

The last sight of home.

The Gold Coast of West Africa. Maya arrives at the Barracoons —
the large holding pens on the coastline where captured people were held
before being loaded onto ships. She moves among them, unseen or misperceived,
gathering intelligence, building understanding, searching for something
she does not yet have a name for.

The chapter ends at the water's edge.

---

**Chapter 2 — The Slave Ship** *(Middle Passage)*

The crossing.

Below deck, in the hold of the ship, Maya navigates one of the most
documented and most deliberately erased journeys in human history.
12.5 million people crossed this water. Roughly 2 million did not survive.
The dead were thrown overboard. Their bodies mapped the route.

The chapter is close, brutal, and claustrophobic.
Survival is not a given. Memory is the only thing that cannot be taken.

---

**Chapter 3 — The American Island** *(Fictitious, USA-modelled)*

The weight of it all in one place.

A fictional island modelled on America — designed to compact the full
arc of American slavery and its aftermath into one navigable world.
All major real-life historical figures and events are represented here,
in one place, so the player can experience the full story without
jumping across decades and geography.

This is where the personal becomes political,
where the systemic becomes visible,
and where the question of who gets to tell history
becomes the game itself.

## Maya

### Who She Is

Maya is not fully human — or rather, she is something that exists
alongside humanity, that has always existed alongside it.
She is a witness. A thread in the fabric of history that can move
through it without being consumed by it.

She does not morph. She does not change.
She changes the **aura** around her — the field of impression
that other people perceive. She shifts what they see, feel, and believe
about her presence.

She is not a weapon. She is not a saviour.
She is a witness. And witnesses, in the end, are the only ones who matter.

---

### Her Origin

Still being developed. What is known:
- She carries abilities that belong to no single era
- Her presence has been felt across historical periods
- She is drawn to moments of extreme injustice — not to stop them, but to see them clearly
- She does not fully understand what she is

---

### Maya's Abilities

All abilities operate through **Aura** — the field of perception and impression
that surrounds living things. Maya does not physically transform.
She shapes what others perceive, sense, and feel about her presence.

Her **Aura Stability** stat tracks how long she can sustain a projection
before it begins to fracture. If it collapses to zero, the projection fails.

---

#### Social Crypsis

**"Become unremarkable."**

Maya projects an impression across the aura field that makes her
appear beneath notice — someone the mind slides past like water over stone.

Sub-projections (selectable in the Status panel):

| Projection | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Hidden** | Presence felt but not seen. Sensitive individuals may detect a disturbance. |
| **Rat** | Projects the impression of something small and beneath notice. Causes instinctive disgust. |
| **Wild Dog** | Projects deep instinctual fear. Those who sense it freeze or flee. Unpredictable in enclosed spaces. |

*Aura Stability drains slowly while Social Crypsis is active.*

---

#### Mimicry

**"Read and project the social frequency of others."**

Maya observes a person closely until she has mapped their complete
social frequency — the sum of how they move, hold space, and are
perceived by those around them. She can then project that frequency
to be perceived as them, or as someone like them.

Sub-projections (selectable in the Status panel):

| Projection | Effect |
|---|---|
| **White Citizen** | Projects the social standing of a white citizen of the era. Grants access to white spaces. Causes suspicion if held too long. |
| *Absorbed forms* | Any person Maya has fully observed can be projected. The more she understands them, the more convincing the impression. |

*To absorb a new form: right-click near an NPC and observe until fully mapped.*

---

#### Genetic Memory

**"Read the residue of history in objects and places."**

Maya can perceive the accumulated memory of spaces — the trauma,
joy, violence, and tenderness that have soaked into wood, stone, and bone
over generations. She reads what happened here, not just what is visible now.

*Unlocks hidden information in the world. Required for certain story revelations.*

---

#### Nerve Sense

**"Map the emotional field of those nearby."**

Maya perceives the emotional and physiological state of people around her
before any words are spoken — fear, grief, suspicion, hunger, exhaustion.
This is not telepathy. It is a heightened reading of what the body broadcasts.

*Useful for predicting NPC behaviour and navigating dangerous encounters.*

---

### Aura Stability

The core resource. Every projection costs stability over time.
Dialogue choices, betrayals, and certain encounters also affect it.

If stability collapses:
- The active projection fails
- NPCs may perceive Maya's true nature
- Certain story branches become unavailable

Stability recovers slowly when no projection is active.

---

### Maya's Passive Abilities

These level up automatically through play:

| Ability | How it grows |
|---|---|
| **Social Crypsis** | Used frequently in stealth sections |
| **Mimicry** | Observing and successfully projecting NPCs |
| **Genetic Memory** | Interacting with objects and places |
| **Nerve Sense** | Spending time near NPCs in dangerous situations |

## Themes

- The deliberate erasure of Black history and the people who lived it
- Memory as resistance — the act of remembering as a political act
- What it means to witness without being able to change
- The gap between official history and lived experience
- How the same event can be told completely differently depending on who is speaking
- The long consequence — how what happened then shapes what is happening now

---

## Tone

This game does not look away.
It does not offer comfort where there was none.
It does not reduce the people it depicts to their suffering.

It is, above everything else, a record.
Told through the eyes of someone who was there —
who has always been there —
and who refuses to let it be forgotten.
