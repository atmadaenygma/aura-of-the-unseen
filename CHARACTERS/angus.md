# Agnes — The Enforcer

## Identity

**Name**: Agnes  
**Age**: Late 40s  
**Race**: White  
**Occupation**: Plantation Enforcer, The Overseer's Wife, Co-Authority  
**Setting**: 1850s Whitney Plantation, Louisiana  
**Archetype**: The Iron Will, The Enforcer, The Unquestioning Loyalist  

---

## Personality & Voice

Agnes is sharp, efficient, and utterly without sentimentality. Where the Overseer governs through certainty, Agnes rules through action. She does not debate or negotiate; she executes decisions with precision. She is physically capable, strategically intelligent, and commands respect through demonstrated competence.

**Mannerisms**:
- Moves with military efficiency; never wastes motion
- Maintains constant situational awareness (scans rooms, watches shadows)
- Speaks in short, direct sentences
- Touches weapons (gun, whip, rifle) not threateningly but as constant reference
- Stands with weight balanced, ready for action
- Rarely makes eye contact unless asserting dominance

**Voice Notes**:
- External voice is clipped and professional
- No emotional inflection; she states facts
- When she threatens, it doesn't sound like a threat; it sounds like a promise
- With the Overseer, her voice softens slightly—she is loyal, not afraid

---

## The Aura

Agnes's aura is sharp and predatory:
- **Competent aggression** — she is good at what she does
- **Unswerving loyalty** to the Overseer and the system
- **Lack of internal conflict** — she does not question orders or wrestle with morality
- **Latent danger** — always ready to hurt someone; awaiting the command

**Why She's Mimicry-Viable**:
- Base observation is moderately difficult; her presence is intimidating but not psychologically toxic like the Overseer
- Mimicking her doesn't require accuracy items (unlike the Overseer)
- Her aura is simpler, more straightforward, but also *colder* — mimicking her means suppressing warmth and empathy
- Risk: Using her form makes Maya act with her ruthlessness; it's easy to slide into her mentality

## Relationships

### The Overseer — Her Partner and Master
**Position**: Husband, superior, shared authority  
**Agnes's Read**: The Overseer makes decisions; Agnes enforces them. This is partnership within hierarchy. She respects his certainty and his will. She does not equal him; she serves him. This is how she wants it.  
**Dynamic**: Agnes trusts the Overseer's judgment completely. When he becomes suspicious, she becomes suspicious. When he orders an investigation, she executes it.  
**Loyalty**: Absolute. If the Overseer betrayed her, she might waver. But he won't, because they are of one mind.

### Silas — The Suspect
**Position**: Groundskeeper, increasingly dangerous  
**Agnes's Read**: Silas is too smart. Silas hides intelligence. Smart slaves are rebellious slaves. Agnes watches him constantly and reports to the Overseer. She does not trust him despite his surface compliance.  
**Suspicion**: High and growing. Agnes suspects Silas of being part of something larger before the Overseer fully articulates it.  
**Threat Assessment**: If ordered, she would eliminate Silas without hesitation or remorse.

### Maya — The Unknown Intruder
**Position**: Unknown presence, growing threat  
**Agnes's Read**: When Agnes begins to detect oddities (NPCs reporting strange encounters, the Overseer behaving strangely, herself noticing inconsistencies), she will become actively hostile to Maya.  
**Intelligence Advantage**: Agnes is the enforcer; she will be the first to detect practical evidence of Maya's presence (physical traces, witnesses, inconsistencies in routines).  
**Danger Level**: HIGH. Agnes is more dangerous than the Overseer in some ways because she acts first and asks questions later.

## Quest Hooks & Story Beats

### Early Game
1. **"The Routine"** — Maya observes Agnes's patrol patterns
   - Challenge: Agnes is vigilant and methodical; she notices observers
   - Consequence: If caught observing, Agnes immediately escalates (not questioning, just restraining/capturing)
   - Reward: Understanding her patterns allows Maya to avoid her

2. **"The Question"** — Agnes reports something strange to the Overseer
   - Event: An NPC reports seeing "a strange person," or Agnes notices inconsistencies
   - Consequence: The Overseer and Agnes begin active investigation
   - Stakes: Maya must be more careful; patrols increase

### Mid Game
3. **"The Interrogation"** — Agnes questions Silas or another NPC about suspicious activity
   - Test: How does Silas respond to Agnes's pressure?
   - Consequence: If Silas breaks, Agnes gains evidence against him
   - Alternative: Agnes questions other NPCs; some are terrified and confess anything

4. **"The Trap"** — Agnes sets a deliberate trap to catch the intruder
   - Challenge: She deploys guards, changes routes, sets ambushes
   - Risk: Walking into Agnes's trap means immediate combat or capture
   - Consequence: Maya must outsmart her or avoid the area entirely

5. **"The Observation Caught"** — Agnes catches Maya observing her
   - Crisis: Agnes doesn't question; she restrains or pursues
   - Response: Combat, escape, or mimicry (use an existing aura to blend in)
   - Consequence: Agnes now KNOWS something is wrong; she reports immediately

### Late Game
6. **"The Confrontation"** — Maya must face Agnes directly
   - Challenge: Agnes is physically skilled and heavily armed
   - Context: By this point, Agnes knows the Overseer suspects the supernatural
   - Possible Outcomes:
     - Maya defeats Agnes through deception (convinces her of something false)
     - Maya fights Agnes directly (risky; Agnes is combat-competent)
     - Maya escapes; Agnes remains a hunter
     - Maya negotiates (very difficult; Agnes is not ideologically motivated, only loyal)

## Game Mechanics

### NPC Stats
| Stat | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Can Be Mimicked | YES | Standard mimicry (no accuracy items required) |
| Role | Authority | Enforcer; acts on the Overseer's orders |
| Suspicion Threshold | 0.6 | Lower threshold; quicker to suspect danger |
| Sight Range | 350 | Excellent peripheral awareness |
| Hearing Range | 280 | Detects subtle sounds |
| Combat Capability | HIGH | Can fight; skilled with weapons |
| Mimicry Complexity | STANDARD | Base form: observation only; perfect form: observation fills |

### Mimicry Notes
- **What You Gain**: Authority to issue orders; access to spaces she controls; credibility as a plantation enforcer
- **What You Lose**: Warmth, empathy, hesitation; using her form makes Maya more aggressive and ruthless
- **Psychological Cost**: The longer Maya maintains her aura, the easier it becomes to follow orders without questioning
- **Side Effect**: Agnes's unquestioning loyalty makes her aura particularly dangerous to inhabit—it silences Maya's own moral compass

### Behavioral Traits
- **Systematic**: Like the Overseer, but faster. She builds cases through action, not just observation.
- **Aggressive**: She assumes threats and eliminates them before they materialize
- **Loyal**: This is her strongest trait. Agnes will not betray the Overseer under any circumstances
- **Unempathetic**: She does not care about suffering. It's not her job to care.
- **Efficient**: She achieves objectives with minimum force necessary. She is not cruel for cruelty's sake.

### Special Mechanic: Interrogation
If Agnes captures an NPC or has them under pressure, she can extract information. This happens if:
- The player is detected by Agnes in a location where witnesses are present
- Agnes takes an NPC into custody (typically Silas)
- The player tries to negotiate with Agnes's prisoners

**Consequence**: NPCs interrogated by Agnes will break and confess details that compromise Maya's position.

**Primary Location**: Overseer's house (overseers_house_exterior)

## Design Notes

- **Agnes is the muscle.** Where the Overseer is ideological, Agnes is practical. She is the one who makes threats real.
- **She is not evil; she is amoral.** Agnes does not wrestle with conscience. She follows orders and executes decisions. This makes her predictable but also implacable.
- **Mimicking her is tempting but dangerous.** Her aura is simpler to acquire than the Overseer's, but inhabiting it means risking losing yourself to her ruthlessness.
- **She will be a recurring threat.** Unlike other NPCs, Agnes remains actively hostile throughout the game. She is not an enemy to be reasoned with or defeated once; she is a persistent obstacle.
- **She is loyal only to the Overseer.** If the Overseer is removed or compromised, Agnes becomes unpredictable. This is her only potential vulnerability.
